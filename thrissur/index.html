Local audience

Who in Thrissur he speaks to

Thrissur is known across Kerala for jewellery and gold. Large jewellery showrooms, gold-loan companies and banks with roots in the city employ branch staff whose daily work is to win a customer's trust and keep it.

Textile and silk shops, home-appliance showrooms and the busy retail around Swaraj Round add a steady stream of floor-sales jobs, and the town has a strong base of family-owned traders.

Engineering, arts and management colleges around Thrissur supply many of these recruits, which makes a short career talk for final-year students a practical way to show where customer-facing work can lead.

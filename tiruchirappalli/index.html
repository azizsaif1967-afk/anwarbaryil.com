Sales trainer in Tiruchirappalli

Tiruchirappalli, or Trichy, is an engineering and college city, home to heavy industry, railway workshops, small engineering units and many institutes. Anwar Barayil, a Chennai-based sales trainer, offers Trichy colleges a 90-minute career talk and offers companies sales team training.

Local audience

Who in Tiruchirappalli he speaks to

Trichy is known for heavy engineering. The boiler and equipment works around Thiruverumbur, the railway workshops at Golden Rock and the many small engineering units that supply them employ large technical teams, and the firms that sell their products need people who understand both the machine and the customer.

The city is also a centre of education, with engineering, arts, science and management colleges that send thousands of graduates into the job market every year. Many of those students see sales only as a last option.

Distributors, retailers and service firms in the city and in the towns around it are the other big employer of sellers, and the FMCG route to the delta districts runs through here.

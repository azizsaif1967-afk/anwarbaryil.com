Six lessons

What he wants students to take home

01 · Decision

Take the pay cut that has a future.

Left a 7-to-2 Etisalat job for a van job selling chocolates in the heat, then took Gillette Riyadh at 25% less. 99.9% said he was mad.

→ Short-term loss, long-term room? Decide, then stop polling people.

02 · The room

Everything in this room was sold.

Fan, clock, desk, the shirt on your back. Someone sold it. Sales is the process in between: products, services, ideas, events, concepts.

→ Name five things you can see and trace who sold each one.

03 · Numbers

Your numbers speak louder than your manager.

A salesman who sells 10 cars a month cannot be passed over for the one who sold 2.

→ Pick a job where your result is a figure on a board.

04 · Resilience

Twelve visits, six months, one box.

An airport grocery refused Bounty every fortnight for six months. His salary was paid for the effort, and there is always a next visit.

→ The sale is in the return visit.

05 · Skills

Listen, verify, then speak.

Obtain, verify, transmit. Know your product and the competitor's. Turn features into benefits. Separate real objections from false ones. Close with the smaller yes.

→ Nobody buys a bag. A trainee buys the bag for her first sales call.

06 · Route

Sales is the fastest road to entrepreneur.

Almost every student said 'entrepreneur'; none could say in what. Sales, then management, then leadership, then your own business.

→ Ask about pay mix, quota and growth plan. Find a mentor who sells.

SMART goals · "the most important lesson of the whole course"

How to write a goal that works

S

Specific

Not 'entrepreneur'. A mobile shop on SM Street, Calicut, 10 lakhs, by 2030.

M

Measurable

10 lakhs in 4 years = 2.5 lakh a year = about 22,000 rupees a month saved.

A

Achievable, yet challenging

30 crores by 2030 is not a goal, it is a wish.

R

Realistic

Then align short-term, long-term and lifetime goals, and write them down.

Homework he set: 1 lifetime goal, 3 long-term goals (3, 5 and 10 years), 5 short-term goals. Post them where you sit, review before the weekend, reward yourself for each one hit, and tell someone who will ask.

  9. 22:59Lesson #2: took the Gillette area-sales-manager job in Riyadh at 25% less pay and sent his family to Chennai for a year — for the future, not the present.
  10. 25:10Core message: 'Many times in life you will have to take tough decisions. Don't look at short-term losses; look at long-term opportunities. Don't listen to people.'
  11. 26:50Two objectives: (1) show a career that is highly rewarding but least prioritised; (2) a 'gift' — one module from his sales training that anyone can use.
  12. 27:47Asks students their life objective. Almost everyone says 'entrepreneur' — none can say in what, or what the first step is.
  13. 31:47'If you want to be a businessman and you are not a salesman yourself, you can't be in business.' Nobody ever says 'I want to be a successful salesperson'.
  14. 35:02Look around the room: fan, clock, desk — every object was sold by someone. Sales is the process in between.
  15. 37:21Why he does this free: ISR (individual social responsibility), sharpening his own training skill, and building a CV of sessions for future paid assignments. He was a founding contributor to SAFI.
  16. 44:26From thousands of interviews: 90% fail because they don't talk, don't think, aren't vibrant — 'that's what I am seeing in this room'.
  17. 41:22You can sell a product, a service, an idea, an event, a concept (digital payments to the Indian government).
  18. 46:30Benefits: every business needs it; skills travel (communication, persuasion, resilience); results are measurable — your numbers speak louder than your manager; sales → management → leadership → entrepreneurship is the fastest route.
  19. 51:33The Bounty story: 12 visits over 6 months to one airport grocery that kept refusing. 'My salary is paid for the effort, not only the sale.' Resilience.
  20. 56:00Integrity (trust is built by selling the right product truthfully) and curiosity (a crawling child grabs, bites, smells — keep that curiosity about the customer's needs).
  21. 58:19Self-discipline: plan the route before leaving home; nobody is watching a salesman in the field.
  22. 59:23Listening before communication. OVT: Obtain, Verify, Transmit — illustrated by the 'main building' mix-up on the way to this venue.
  23. 1:02:32Persuasion demo with his own bag: '200 rupees, will you buy?' → No. Re-pitched to a student as the sample bag she'll need in her first sales job → the odds change. Sell benefits to a need.
  24. 1:04:17Product knowledge incl. competitors (Activa vs Bajaj vs Suzuki). Features = characteristics; benefits = what it does for the buyer. First correct answer of the day.
  25. 1:05:43Objection handling: real vs false objections. Prioritising: lead with the best seller (Bounty) to get into the store, then widen the range.
  26. 1:07:29Closing: alternative close ('don't take 15, take the 5 fast movers') and small-commitment close ('two boxes, 60 dirhams').
  27. 1:09:02Pay (via ChatGPT): India fresher 3–5 LPA, mid-level 8–10 LPA, senior ~2 lakh/month.
  28. 1:09:42Gulf: 3,000–7,000 Dh entry, ~22,000 Dh sales-manager average; he knows people on 100,000 Dh/month commission. Break.
  29. 1:12:15Framed on Stephen Covey's habits — begin with the end in mind. He had 2 hours of material and 15 minutes left because the students' bus leaves at 3:30.
  30. 1:13:37Football analogy: remove the goal posts and 22 players just run around exhausting themselves with no sense of achievement. That's a life without goals.
  31. 1:14:29Three levels: lifetime goal, long-term goals, short-term goals. Students' lifetime goals: 'be happy', 'travel and make parents proud', 'be a good person'.
  32. 1:16:37A lifetime goal is a compass: if yours is heaven, you won't lie or cheat; if yours is happiness, rewrite it as 'I and everyone who deals with me is happy'.
  33. 1:17:47SMART goals — 'the most important lesson of the entire course': Specific ('mobile shop on SM Street, Calicut, 10 lakhs by 2030' not 'entrepreneur'), Measurable (10 lakhs in 4 years = 2.5 lakh/yr = 22,000/month saved), Achievable-yet-challenging, Realistic.
  34. 1:21:11Homework: write 1 lifetime goal, 3 long-term goals (3/5/10 yrs), 5 short-term goals; check each against SMART; align short → long → lifetime.
  35. 1:22:20Write them down, post them on your desk or fridge, review often, change only if truly needed, reward yourself when you hit one, and share them with someone who will follow up.
  36. 1:23:13Asks students to WhatsApp/email him their goals and, at month-end, what they achieved — 'that will be the best gift to me'.
  37. 1:23:54Feedback form handed out: 24 students responded and rated the overall event 8.5 out of 10.
  38. 1:24:42Next step for would-be entrepreneurs: look at sales roles in structured companies, ask about pay mix, quota and growth plan, find a mentor who sells for a living.

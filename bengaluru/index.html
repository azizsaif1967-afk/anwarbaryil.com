Local audience

Who in Bengaluru he speaks to

Bengaluru is the centre of India's technology and startup work, and almost every product company there needs people who can sell it. Software firms, SaaS startups and IT services companies run inside sales and account teams that are often staffed by young graduates new to the job.

Alongside the tech sector the city has a long list of business schools and engineering colleges, and a steady retail and consumer goods trade around its wide market. Final-year students there are often choosing between a technical role and a first sales role.

Anwar speaks to both groups: students who have not yet seen sales as a career, and early sales staff who want a clear method for finding customers, handling objections and following up.

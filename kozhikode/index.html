Local audience

Who in Kozhikode he speaks to

Kozhikode, also called Calicut, is the trading heart of Malabar. Timber, hardware, textiles, spices and gold have been sold here for generations, and the family-run shops and wholesalers around Mittai Theruvu and Palayam still set the pace of local business.

The city is also a student town. Management programmes, engineering colleges and the national institutes around Kozhikode send many graduates into sales, banking and distribution roles across Malabar and the Gulf.

Anwar knows this market from the inside. After his years in the Gulf he built his own distribution business across Kerala and Tamil Nadu, so he talks to Kozhikode traders and students as someone who has done the same work.

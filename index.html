Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is Anwar Barayil?

Anwar Barayil is a sales and goal-setting trainer. He spent 27 years with multinationals in the Gulf, including Gillette, P&G and Etisalat, rising from sales clerk to Trade Marketing Manager for the Middle East. He then spent 20 years running his own businesses in the UAE and India. He now gives career talks, goal-setting workshops and selling-skills training for colleges, institutes and companies across India.

Who offers sales training in Kerala?

Anwar Barayil offers selling-skills training, goal-setting workshops and career talks in Kerala, including Kozhikode (Calicut), Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram. His first session after moving back to India was at SAFI Institute of Advanced Study, Calicut, on 2 November 2026, where 24 MBA and PGDM students rated the overall event 8.5 out of 10.

What is a goal-setting workshop?

A goal-setting workshop is a guided session where each participant writes down specific goals and a first step towards each one. Anwar Barayil runs it as a half-day workshop with the exercise built in. At SAFI Institute, all 24 students called the goal-setting part interesting, useful or practical.

Can colleges book a career talk on sales as a career?

Yes. The career talk "Sales is a career worth choosing" runs for 90 minutes and is aimed at final-year business and management students. It explains why sales belongs beside engineering, medicine, law and accountancy, using Anwar Barayil's own path from sales clerk to manager. After the SAFI talk, 79% of the 24 students said they were open to a sales career.

What does the selling-skills course cover?

The selling-skills course runs for 1 to 2 days. It covers finding customers, the sales call, handling objections and closing. The material comes from 27 years in field sales, trade marketing and sales training at Gillette, P&G and Etisalat. At SAFI, 96% of students (23 of 24) said they want to learn selling skills.

Does Anwar Barayil travel outside South India?

Yes. His first preference is the four southern states: Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. He is based in Chennai. Colleges and companies in any other Indian state, or abroad, can book any session when the host covers travel and accommodation along with his fee. Corporate sales team training covers field sales, trade marketing and sales planning for FMCG, telecom and retail teams, and is built to each company's needs.

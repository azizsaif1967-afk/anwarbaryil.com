Local audience

Who in Kochi he speaks to

Kochi is a working port city, and much of its business is moving, selling and distributing goods. Shipping and port-linked firms, logistics companies and the wholesalers who supply the whole district all depend on people who can sell and follow up.

The IT parks at Infopark and around Kakkanad employ young graduates who are good at their craft and often unsure how to talk to a customer. Retail around Edappally and MG Road, and the FMCG distributors who feed it, are the other big group of sales jobs in the city.

Kochi also has a long list of MBA and management colleges, so a career talk for final-year students fits easily into a normal teaching week.

Your students

Why it fits Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi has federal and private universities, international branch campuses and applied colleges. Many of their business and engineering graduates join banks, energy firms, telecom and government-linked companies, where client-facing and selling skills decide who moves up.

Many students rule out sales before they understand it. Anwar shows them, through his own 27 years in Gulf companies, how a first sales job can lead to management, and what the first year really looks like.
